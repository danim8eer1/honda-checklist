Dealer playbook

Lines to have ready. Walk in armored.

🛡 your opening line
What to say when you walk in "I saw the CR-V Hybrid Sport-L advertised at $238.88 with $3,988 at signing for 36 months. I'd like to see the units available at that price."
🚩 if they say the deal is gone
Common tactic — don't panic Say: "That's fine, show me something comparable at a similar effective monthly." Then hold your ~$335 effective monthly target. The Honda Financial lease program is statewide — check Norm Reeves, Irvine Auto Center, or any OC dealer if this one won't play ball.
💬 lines to have ready
On price "I've done my research on Edmunds and CarsDirect. I know what this car leases for in California."
On add-ons "I'm going to pass on all the add-ons today, thank you." — Say it once, don't explain, don't engage.
On the four-square "I want to agree on the lease price first before we talk about the trade-in. Let's keep them separate."
On pressure "I need to take this home and review it tonight." — Your escape hatch. Use it freely.
On the Camry trade "CarMax offered me $X. Can you beat that?" — Have the written offer in hand. If they can't beat it, sell to CarMax. Simple.
⚠️ the f&i office — danger zone
What to expect After the lease is agreed, they walk you to the Finance & Insurance office. This person's entire job is add-ons. They will present a menu of products, some bundled together. Your answer to everything except gap insurance is "no thank you." The bumper-to-bumper warranty covers the entire lease — an extended warranty is pure profit for them.
🏆 the golden rule

You can leave at any time for any reason. A CR-V Hybrid will be on the lot next weekend. The deal that exists today exists again next month. Never let urgency be manufactured for you.

📌 Your advantages going in You have the ad screenshot · You know the out-the-door number · You have a CarMax offer in hand · You have clean credit (740) · You have clean driving record · You are not in a rush. That's a stronger position than 95% of buyers.
